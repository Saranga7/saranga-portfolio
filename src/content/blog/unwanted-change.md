<p style="text-align:center;">As I sit at my study<br />
I glance without, often.<br />
Through the fleur grills of my window<br />
my eyes sight the world.</p>
<p style="text-align:center;">My gaze wanders about<br />
the worldly frame, guided<br />
by the magpies’ chirps<br />
and raindrop taps, till<br />
they fix upon the maple,<br />
miles away on the mountain<br />
‘neath a clear blue sky.</p>
<p style="text-align:center;">And as the days go by,<br />
my eyes wander much,<br />
eventually unguided<br />
to find the tree amidst the green:<br />
Gaze hindered by rising giants<br />
of firm sand, shaped by human hand<br />
‘neath a desolate sky.</p>
<p style="text-align:center;">And when that day comes,<br />
I shall draw the curtains and go away.</p>
