<p style="text-align:center">Up above<br>
‘neath starry skies, my home lies;<br>
Far away from this land of hate,<br>
for ages and ages my home awaits.</p>
<p style="text-align:center">Wonder how I ended in the present<br>
where the fiery-frosty rain<br>
rules terror and thunders pain,<br>
And Leo’s stead is the murky moon crescent.</p>
<p style="text-align:center">The land is plain but not its people,<br>
with their own vague vexation:<br>
A rose in one hand<br>
the other with a sickle-<br>
To plant the rose in sand,<br>
or swing the sickle to temptation.</p>
<p style="text-align:center">Oh, how I long for that home in the hill,<br>
covered by trees of oak and pine,<br>
a clear blue sky with cirrus still,<br>
amidst the sweet cherry blossoms.<br>
In the land where neighbors are men<br>
sans <em>Vain Thoughts</em><br>
and no vows broken.</p>
<p style="text-align:center">You may visit when you yen.<br>
You don’t know where it lies,<br>
and nor do I<br>
So, better leave vague ventures.</p>
<p style="text-align:center">But when cicadas will roar<br>
from my doors of paper,<br>
and flakes of snow and petal fall.<br>
When my ears’ll hear the stream’s tone<br>
I will have found my Home.</p>
