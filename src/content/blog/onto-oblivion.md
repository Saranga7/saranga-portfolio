<p style="text-align:center;">Autumn days, auburn twilights<br />I sit myself often<br />on a rocking chair, crafted<br />by my beloved’s hands<br />That nurse the overlooking orchids,<br />Strolling along my vivid<br />Lane of memories, hued and shaded;<br />Myriads of constellations<br />canopying the path.</p>
<p style="text-align:center;">One fine day, we sit on the porch,<br />His contrived fingers<br />caressing my shoulders,<br />my curious lips utter, “How’s Yuri?”<br />He smiles with sad eyes,<br />But as his eyes glance upon<br />my blank face, his face shades grim.</p>
<p style="text-align:center;">My lane is now a labyrinth<br />outgrowing obscured turns.<br />I wander the same unknown paths<br />greeted by many untold smiles,<br />a few stars shedding light.</p>
<p style="text-align:center;">And on one wintry morning,<br />A man lay calm on the cold wooden floor,<br />My hands holding his moulded fingers,<br />His lifeless eyes did look familiar.<br />I lift my gaze on the mirror forth-<br />a withered woman<br />gazing me with empty eyes.</p>
