<p>Dear Departed,<br />
I envy your wisdom,<br />
your unheeding eyes<br />
glaring stark oblivion.<br />
What deemed you so worthy?</p>
<p>They say, dead men tell no tales.<br />
No, they tell a thousand tales,<br />
knitting their own truths-<br />
truths construed, truths contemplated<br />
but truths uncertain.</p>
<p>Tell me, O’ Departed<br />
Is it true, what Sir Simon said?<br />
Is the truth so simple<br />
that your eyes teared when you died?<br />
Is death so peaceful?<br />
Wouldn’t you come and tempt me?</p>
<p>Just once.</p>
