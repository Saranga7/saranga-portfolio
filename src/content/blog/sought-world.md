<p style="text-align:center;">The world I seek is simple.<br />
I see beast and man,<br />
relishing hand in hand.<br />
Where Alps and Fuji<br />
are ever-nigh neighbours,<br />
feet upon the same land.<br />
Where the Nile and the Ganges<br />
haven’t forgotten that Tiber<br />
is their own lost brother,<br />
from the One Faith- Nature.</p>
<p>Where lips may move with flaw,<br />
but hearts sing the same song<br />
And there is no wrong<br />
in a little leisure,<br />
or some sacred pleasure.</p>
<p>In this reality that I seek, I see<br />
men are more than what they create,<br />
and the latter ne’er dominate.<br />
Where hope is not a false deed.<br />
This world in which love is forever<br />
will soon be,<br />
for what I seek is simple indeed.</p>
