<p style="text-align: center">On the eleventh ember,<br />
the seventh night<br />
A soul did render<br />
and irides saw light.</p>
<p style="text-align: center">From stride to step,<br />
And step to leap<br />
from pride to help<br />
And help to lead.</p>
<p style="text-align: center">With every mile I go<br />
and every turn I take;<br />
several seas to row<br />
few feasts to make.</p>
<p style="text-align: center">Many dogs to meet<br />
and women to like.<br />
Through frost and heat,<br />
A hundred hills to hike.</p>
<p style="text-align: center">Greedy gambles to bet,<br />
Iridescent impossibilities.<br />
Twelve tigers to pet;<br />
half a legion of lies.</p>
<p style="text-align: center">Worthy soils to spade.<br />
Seven sins to burn.<br />
Plenty ponds to wade<br />
And many men to mourn.</p>
<p style="text-align: center">Until that very day<br />
when the dawn meets dusk<br />
unto the last ray<br />
Out this mortal husk.</p>
