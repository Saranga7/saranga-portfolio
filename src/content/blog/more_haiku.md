A tissue for her
White, blue or polka-dotted
doesn't much matter.

# Desolate
Amidst ash and bone
awaits a desolate throne
I won, but did I?

# Dictionary
Vocabulary
needs dictionaries like a
whetstone to a sword.

# Hunting
To wield or to yield
Choose glory or choose to fade.
Hunt or be hunted.

# Win
Plan your trickery-
Hide your men within the horse.
Winning is winning.

# Bear
Two boys in the woods,
one lied prone, the other ran;
Now, this bear eats the dead.

# Progressive
From TQDM,
I do love a progress bar.
One per epoch, please.

# Acquaintance
Summer acquaintance,
Love is in the autumn air,
Winter enemy.

# Thought
What's faster than light?
Across quarks and galaxies,
Top-down, start to end.

# Leaflet
"Word of God," said he:
Leaflet of sins and penance,
I committed all.

# Waiter
A suit and a bow.
His uniform for the night,
appreciates tips.

# Stock
Stock all while you can,
the siege shall begin at dawn.
Hail the Gods, save us!

# Wavelet 
You're a ripple in 
the grand flow of the cosmos-
Wavelet of a wink.

# Banish
Burned and banished prince,
with wise Uncle and a ship;
Changed and found himself.

# Prove
Hear the battlecry?
Dreaded soldiers, proven prince.
Don't forget your blade.

# Withdrawn
She sang the wrong song.
Now withdrawn and desparate,
back to the shadows.

# Institution
Constraints, frameworks, walls.
To institutionalize
body, soul, spirit.

# Golf
Three Sundays from now
golf or soccer, matter naught.
Let's go out and play.

# Chant
I hear hums and chants,
Echoing from old chambers,
A death conjuring.

# Music
Bird chirps, flowing streams;
Body healer, soul cleanser-
music of nature.

# Rhythm
Dum dum of the drum,
spirit of freedom and pride-
A march in rhythm.

# Lament
Behold, wield your sword!
Battle for love or power?
Child and bride laments.

# Roar
Hear me roar, my child
To victory or glory-
rival tides are here.

# Linger
The war is over.
Blood shed, thirst quenched, throne won.
Death and grief lingers.
