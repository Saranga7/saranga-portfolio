Saranga worked on automatic musical-instrument recognition and classification using audio features and neural networks.

The research used mel-frequency cepstral coefficients to represent instrument audio, exploring both deep neural networks and convolutional neural networks for classification.

The associated article, *Exploiting Cepstral Coefficients and CNN for Efficient Musical Instrument Classification*, was published in *Evolving Systems* in 2023.

An earlier related study, *Deep Neural Network for Musical Instrument Recognition using MFCCs*, was published in *Computación y Sistemas* in 2021.
