## Elemental blog themes

Courgette, Caveat, Metal Mania, and Bitter are bundled from the
[Google Fonts repository](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl)
under the SIL Open Font License. Their licences accompany each font in
`public/assets/fonts/`. Previously bundled Freckle Face, Lora and Cinzel remain available
but are no longer selected by the elemental themes.

The optional four-nation themes are an unofficial fan tribute to
*Avatar: The Last Airbender*. Avatar and its nation-symbol designs belong to
their respective rights holders, including Nickelodeon. The nation symbols
come from the site-owner-supplied `atla/elemental_symbols.jpg` sheet. SVG
clipping and luminance-to-alpha masks preserve the supplied shapes while
removing their tile backgrounds. The four landscape PNGs were also supplied
in `atla/`; responsive WebP versions are generated locally at build time.
Their original creator and licence were not provided; no open-licence or
ownership claim is made. See [asset notes](public/assets/elements/CREDITS.txt),
also linked from the theme picker. No affiliation is implied.

The combined toggle artwork, `atla.png`, was supplied by Saranga Mahanta
and identified by him as AI-generated. It replaces the earlier DeviantArt
image. CSS applies contrast/blending and dark-mode inversion; the supplied
image itself is unchanged. The previous Commons emblems and hand-drawn
landscape SVGs have been retired.

## Dali curiosity library

The previous bulk collection has been replaced by 1,000 short, source-linked
facts paraphrased in original wording. Each speech card links to its
source article; these are references, not endorsements of the portfolio.

- [BBC Science Focus](https://www.sciencefocus.com/science/fun-facts)
- [Concordia University Texas](https://www.concordia.edu/blog/35-random-fascinating-facts.html)
- [Arunia on Medium](https://medium.com/@prouspwhs/30-random-unhinged-and-slightly-cool-facts-ive-collected-7baeb3b40a51)
- [The Fact Site](https://www.thefactsite.com/1000-interesting-facts/)

The expanded selection also references NASA, the San Diego Zoo Wildlife
Alliance, the US National Park Service, UNESCO's World Heritage Centre,
the Computer History Museum, and Wikipedia. Exact article titles and URLs
are stored with every entry. Article text, photographs and illustrations
have not been republished; entries use independently worded factual statements.

The requested Reader's Digest and TODAY articles could not be retrieved during
this review and were not used. Selection favours self-contained, accessible
facts over technical catalog entries or unsupported superlatives. The library
has a minimum of 1,000 entries, without padding individual category quotas.
Additions require a direct source link and the automated corpus checks.
